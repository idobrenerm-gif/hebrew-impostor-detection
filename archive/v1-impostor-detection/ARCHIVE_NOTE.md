# Archive: v1 — Hebrew impostor detection

This folder is a frozen snapshot of the project's first direction, taken on 2026-11-11 when the project pivoted (see `docs/DECISIONS.md`, D-012).

- **v1 idea**: detect LLM-generated content from foreign influence operations impersonating Israelis on Hebrew social media (binary: impostor vs. authentic).
- **Why it was dropped**: after the proposal presentation, the lecturer pointed out that the intent to impersonate cannot be inferred from the text alone, so the task is much weaker than plain "AI vs. not AI".
- **Contents**: the docs, README and CLAUDE.md as they were before the pivot, and the v1 proposal deck (`slides/`).

Do not edit files in this folder. The live decision and progress logs continue in `docs/`.
